I am creating a website for a client, a hair booking platform that salons can onboard onto and manage their shop on, also clients will be able to pick a service, then pick a salon near them. The website should enable clients to:

- book appointments (& pay a deposit at the point of booking)
- customise their service (style, colour, length) with a dynamically updating visual preview (updated every time a new style/colour etc. is chosen)

We will provide a list of pre-set services (Braids & Protective Styles, Wig Installs, Natural Hair Care, Locs & Twists, Treatments), that a user (salon client) will pick from when onboarding, they will also upload portfolio pictures that will be used on their platform.
